隐私政策

本政策适用于 Fenrir Chengdu Inc. 提供的“喝了么”（Heleme）iPhone App 及配套 Apple Watch App。

1. 我们如何处理数据

App 无需注册，不接入广告、第三方追踪或行为分析服务。我们不要求姓名、通讯录、位置或照片权限，也不运行接收你饮水记录的自有服务器。

你主动记录的饮水量、时间、记录来源及偏好设置，用于显示进度、历史回顾、提醒和设备同步，首先保存在设备的 App 存储中。App 会保留必要的记录编号、修改版本和删除标记，防止重复计数或恢复已删除记录。

2. Apple Watch 与通知

配对设备通过 Apple 的 Watch Connectivity 机制交换所需记录和设置。临时断连时，手表保留待同步记录。手机保存确认后，手表移除待确认项。卸载手表 App 可能丢失尚未同步的记录。

通知由 iOS 在本机安排，需你允许权限。系统决定通知显示在手机还是手表，专注模式、系统调度和设备状态可能影响提示。App 不使用自有推送服务器收集设备推送令牌。

3. 可选的 Apple 健康

只有你主动开启并授权后，App 才向 Apple 健康写入本 App 的饮水量。为避免重复、同步修改或删除、以及在你选择恢复时找回记录，App 会查询自己曾写入的饮水条目；不申请读取其他 App 的饮水记录或其他健康指标。

手机统一处理手机、通知操作和收到的手表记录。修改与删除以本 App 为准。关闭同步会停止后续同步，但不会自动删除已经写入健康的记录；你可在健康 App 中管理它们。

Apple 健康的云端同步和备份由 Apple 的系统设置与政策管理，与本 App 的 iCloud 设置备份分开。我们无法代你开启系统健康备份，也不保证健康数据立即出现在另一台设备上。

4. 可选的 iCloud 设置备份

开启后，仅将目标、杯量、提醒时段和间隔等偏好，以及用于辨认备份来源的随机设备编号和备份时间，保存到你 Apple 账户的 CloudKit 私有数据库。新备份不包含饮水记录或健康样本。开发者不会通过自有服务器接收这些内容。

旧测试版本曾提供饮水记录的私有云备份。新版本不再写入该旧备份；如存在，你可先导入所需记录、确认健康同步，再通过设置中的删除入口明确删除。关闭自动备份或卸载 App 不会自动清除已有云端备份。

5. 你的选择与删除

你可拒绝或撤销通知与健康权限，关闭健康同步或 iCloud 备份，并继续使用本机记录功能。在历史明细左滑可删除记录；健康同步开启且权限有效时，App 会尝试删除自己写入的对应健康条目，失败时保留待处理状态。

你也可在 Apple 健康和系统 iCloud 存储管理中管理数据。卸载 App 会移除该设备的 App 本地数据，系统备份的处理遵循 Apple 设置；不会保证同时删除健康、iCloud 或另一台设备上的数据。换机或抹掉设备前，请在新设备确认需要的记录已恢复。

6. 网站、联系与第三方

打开饮水建议出处链接会访问相应网站，适用该网站的隐私政策。本支持网站不嵌入分析、广告或追踪脚本；网站托管服务提供方 GitHub 可能根据其政策处理访问请求相关信息。你通过邮件联系支持时，邮件内容由你的邮件服务和我们的支持邮箱处理；请勿发送不必要的健康、身份或账户敏感信息。

Apple 和 GitHub 的平台服务受各自隐私政策约束。本 App 不出售记录，不将其用于广告或用户画像。若处理方式改变，我们会更新本政策，并在需要时重新征得许可。

7. 使用范围与联系

本 App 用于饮水习惯记录，不提供诊断、治疗或个体化医疗建议。参考目标主要面向健康成年人，食物水分不计入记录目标；儿童及需要限制饮水的人群应遵循监护人或专业医护建议，不应为了完成进度环集中大量饮水。

Privacy policy

This policy covers Heleme for iPhone and its companion Apple Watch app, provided by Fenrir Chengdu Inc.

1. Local records and purpose

No account is required. The app has no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not request your name, contacts, location or photos, and we do not operate a server that receives your water records.

Water amounts, times, record origins and preferences you enter are stored in the app's device storage for progress, history, reminders and device synchronization. Stable identifiers, edit versions and deletion markers prevent duplicates and unwanted restoration.

2. Apple Watch and notifications

Paired devices exchange needed records and settings through Apple's Watch Connectivity. Unsynced records remain on the watch until the phone acknowledges them. Removing the watch app can lose records not yet transferred.

iOS schedules local notifications after you grant permission. System settings determine whether alerts appear on iPhone or Apple Watch. Focus, scheduling and device state can affect delivery. We do not collect push tokens through a push server.

3. Optional Apple Health

Only after you opt in and authorize access does the app save its water records to Apple Health. It queries only entries it originally saved to avoid duplicates, update or remove entries, and restore its own records when you explicitly choose. It does not request access to other apps' water records or unrelated health metrics.

The phone writes records from the phone, notification actions and paired watch. App edits and deletions are authoritative. Turning sync off stops subsequent synchronization without automatically deleting existing Health entries. You can manage these in Health.

System Health cloud sync and backup are managed by Apple and are separate from the app's iCloud preferences backup. We cannot enable them for you or guarantee immediate availability on another device.

4. Optional iCloud preferences backup

When enabled, only preferences such as your goal, cup size, reminder hours and interval, plus a random backup-source device identifier and backup time, go to your Apple account's private CloudKit database. New backups contain no water records or Health samples. The developer does not receive them through an app server.

Earlier test versions offered a private water-record backup. New versions no longer write to it. If present, you can import needed records, confirm Health sync and explicitly remove the legacy backup in Settings. Turning backup off or uninstalling does not automatically clear existing cloud backups.

5. Your controls

You may deny or revoke notification and Health permissions, disable sync and backup, and continue recording locally. Swipe a history entry to delete it. With Health sync and permission enabled, the app also attempts to delete its corresponding Health entry; failed work is retried.

Manage Health and cloud data in Health and Apple's storage settings. Uninstalling removes that device's app data subject to Apple's system-backup behavior; it does not guarantee deletion from Health, iCloud or another device. Confirm records on your new device before erasing the old one.

6. External services and contact

Opening a reference visits its website under that site's policy. This support website embeds no analytics, ads or tracking scripts. Hosting provider GitHub may process request information under its own policy. Support emails are handled by your email service and our support mailbox; avoid sending unnecessary health, identity or account information.

Apple and GitHub services follow their respective policies. We do not sell records or use them for advertising or profiling. Material changes will be reflected here, with renewed permission where needed.

7. Scope and contact

Heleme is a habit tracker, not a diagnosis, treatment or personalized medical service. Reference goals are primarily for healthy adults and exclude food water. Children and people with fluid restrictions should follow appropriate caregiver or medical advice. Do not drink excessively to complete the ring.

For privacy or data questions, contact Fenrir Chengdu Inc. at support@fenrir-inc.com.cn.

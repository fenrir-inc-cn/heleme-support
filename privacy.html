隐私政策

1. 适用范围与本机记录

本政策适用于 Fenrir Chengdu Inc. 的喝了么（Heleme）iPhone、Apple Watch、Mac App 与配套小组件。功能以安装版本及平台为准。iPhone 与 Mac 版 4.0 均提供三种界面风格；可爱和清新风格支持水滴语音及喝水音效，具体互动因平台而异。无需注册，不接入广告、第三方追踪或行为分析，不运行接收用户记录的开发者服务器。App 不申请姓名、通讯录、位置、照片或麦克风权限，不使用拍照识别或外部 AI 分析记录。

本机保存主动记录的饮品种类、容量、时间、来源、编辑状态，以及目标、历史目标、快捷杯量、常用饮品、收藏、提醒与反馈偏好。起身活动由手动确认，不读取运动传感器判定站立。编号、版本、删除标记和已处理事件用于去重、恢复及合并。

2. 通知与最近提醒朗读

通知在本机安排，需要系统权限；App 不运行自有推送服务器。提醒开关、暂停、安排账本和优化状态有设备本地部分。专注模式、通知摘要、后台机会和系统队列影响送达，不能保证精确或长期无人打开时持续提醒。

为复述最近提醒，iPhone 会读取属于本 App 的已送达通知，并在本地保存最新一条的标题、正文、分类、标识和时间。只朗读最近 24 小时内有效提醒；测试通知不参与。最近提醒朗读副本不上传开发者服务器，不加入 App 的跨设备记录同步或 JSON 备份。

3. 外观、水滴语音与音效

标准、可爱、清新风格使用相同记录和业务功能。清新场景根据设备本地时间变化，不读取位置。水滴语音与喝水音效使用随 App 提供的离线音频，运行时不调用在线语音合成、音色克隆或录音服务，不采集用户声音。

语音和音效可分别关闭；未支持语音的语言仍可使用其他功能及喝水音效。界面风格、外观模式和音频开关按设备保留，不参加 iCloud 跨设备记录同步；iPhone 导出的完整 JSON 备份会包含这些偏好，确认导入成功后恢复。旧备份没有这些字段时保留本机当前偏好；可同步的伴随主题与简洁反馈偏好按已开启的 iCloud 功能处理。减少动态效果、减少透明度及系统静音等设置可能影响表现。

4. 成长、洞察与历史目标

奖章、每周回顾和近两周洞察由设备上的记录计算，不是健康评分。洞察使用今天之前的 14 天；未记录不等于未喝水。奖章与达标信息会随记录更正而更新。

从 4.0 起，每日适用目标及升级时的目标基准存入 App 数据，参加 iCloud 跨设备同步和完整 JSON 备份。缺少目标的过去日期使用升级到 4.0 时的目标估算，并在页面说明；估算不代表已找回当时的真实目标。提醒账本、时段优化及效果观察仍保存在本机，不属于完整记录备份。

5. 可选 Apple 健康

在 iPhone 主动开启并授权后，App 协调自己写入 Apple 健康的白水记录，包括从 Watch 或其他设备同步收到的白水。茶、咖啡、牛奶、果汁与其他饮品不写入 Apple 健康。健康数据不用于广告、画像或出售。

白水同步只读取协调本 App 写入的白水样本所需的信息，不把 Apple 健康当作所有饮品的完整备份。关闭同步不自动删除已存在的健康样本；删除 App 记录时，会在权限允许的情况下尝试删除对应自有样本，失败可稍后重试。Mac 不直接访问 Apple 健康。

你也可以分别允许读取站立小时和步数，用于本周活动回顾及选用的健康辅助提醒。App 不向 Apple 健康写入活动数据。每天的站立小时和步数摘要只保存在本机，不参与 App 的 iCloud 同步、手表同步或文件备份。在设置 → Apple 健康中点「停止使用活动数据」会清除本机摘要。手动标记的起身活动属于独立的 App 记录。

6. 可选 iCloud 与 Watch 同步

在 iPhone 或 Mac 开启跨设备同步后，饮品记录、历史目标、记录合并元数据、目标与杯量、提醒偏好、常用与收藏饮品、可同步的伴随偏好、奖章确认、回顾起点和手动起身活动存入你的 Apple 账户的 CloudKit 私有数据库。开发者不通过自有服务器接收或出售这些内容。健康数据库样本本身不作为 App 文档上传。

Watch 通过配对 iPhone 交换记录、所需设置和近期目标历史，不是独立 CloudKit 客户端。离线待确认事件保留在手表；抹掉手表前请确认已同步。iCloud 依赖相同 Apple 账户、网络和系统运行机会；切换账户时同步暂停，关闭同步不删除本机或已有云端数据。

旧设置备份不同于完整记录同步，不包含全部记录。尚未成功完成新版同步的设备可检查或恢复旧备份；成功后该旧版区域隐藏，但不会删除旧云端备份。云端数据由系统 iCloud 存储管理；删除前先关闭相关设备同步并另存备份。

7. 本机恢复、导出与删除

本机恢复副本不能替代设备外备份，也不保证卸载、抹掉或丢失设备后可用。读取失败时保留原始数据，不以空记录覆盖；恢复可能缺少副本时间之后的更改。

完整 JSON 备份包含饮品记录、历史目标、设置、伴随偏好、活动及合并元数据；iPhone 文件另含界面风格、外观模式、水滴语音与喝水音效开关。导入先预览再确认，按标识与版本合并并应用备份设置。CSV 是报表，不能恢复完整数据。导出文件不设额外 App 密码或加密，保存和分享由系统与所选服务处理；删除 App 内记录不会删除导出文件。最近成功导出时间只存本机，不记录所选文件位置，也不证明文件仍存在。

可撤销权限、关闭同步和提醒，继续本机记录。删除状态可参与云端合并，但不会立即从所有备份物理清除。卸载不会保证清除 Apple 健康、iCloud、其他设备或导出文件；抹掉旧设备前，请在新设备核实数据。

8. 小组件、第三方与联系

App 与本机组件通过 App Group 保存显示和快捷记录所需快照，如总量、目标、杯量、饮品、收藏、语言、主题和更新时间。锁屏、桌面及表盘可能向旁人显示记录；刷新由系统控制。

出处链接由相应网站处理。本站不嵌入追踪或分析脚本；GitHub 和 Apple 服务可能按各自政策处理请求。联系支持时邮件由你的邮件服务与支持邮箱处理，请遮挡截图敏感信息，不发送密码或不必要的完整记录。隐私问题请联系 support@fenrir-inc.com.cn。

9. 使用范围

喝了么是习惯记录工具，不提供诊断、治疗或个体化医疗建议。目标、参考量和提醒不是医学安全线。儿童及需要限制饮水者应遵循监护人或专业医护建议，不为完成圆环集中大量饮水。通知不能作为必须准时送达的医疗提醒。

Privacy policy

1. Scope and local records

This policy covers Heleme by Fenrir Chengdu Inc. on iPhone, Apple Watch, Mac and companion widgets. Features vary by version and platform. Heleme 4.0 on iPhone and Mac offers three visual styles. Cute and Fresh include droplet speech and drinking sounds, with interactions that vary by platform. No account registration, ads, third-party tracking or behavioral analytics are used. The developer runs no server receiving your records. The app does not request your name, contacts, location, photos or microphone, and does not analyze records through external AI or camera recognition.

Local data includes logged drink types, volumes, times, sources and edits; goals and goal history; quick cups, usual drinks, favorites, reminders and feedback preferences. Movement breaks are manually confirmed, not detected with motion sensors. Identifiers, revisions, tombstones and handled events support deduplication, recovery and merging.

2. Notifications and recent-reminder speech

Notifications are scheduled locally with system permission, without a developer push server. Some reminder switches, pauses, ledgers and optimization state are device-local. Focus, notification summaries, background opportunities and queue limits affect delivery. Exact or indefinite unattended delivery is not guaranteed.

To repeat a recent reminder, iPhone reads delivered notifications belonging to this app and locally retains the latest valid title, body, category, identifier and time. Only reminders from the last 24 hours qualify; test notifications are excluded. This speech copy is not sent to a developer server or included in app record sync or JSON backups.

3. Appearance, droplet speech and sounds

Standard, Cute and Fresh share records and business features. Fresh scenes follow the device clock without location access. Droplet speech and drinking sounds use bundled offline audio. The running app does not use online speech synthesis, voice cloning or recording services, and does not collect your voice.

Speech and drinking sounds can be disabled separately. Languages without droplet speech retain other features and drinking sounds. Visual style, appearance mode and audio switches remain device-specific and are not included in iCloud record sync. Full JSON files exported on iPhone include these preferences and restore them after a successful confirmed import. Older backups without these fields leave current local preferences unchanged; syncable companion themes and simple-feedback preferences follow enabled iCloud features. Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency and system silent mode can affect presentation.

4. Growth, insights and historical goals

Medals, weekly recaps and two-week insights are computed on-device from records, not health scores. Insights use the 14 days before today; missing records do not prove no drinking. Corrections can change medals and goal results.

Starting in 4.0, daily applicable goals and the upgrade goal baseline are part of app data, iCloud record sync and full JSON backups. Missing goals for earlier dates use the goal at the 4.0 upgrade as an explicitly disclosed estimate, not recovered proof of the actual historical target. Reminder ledgers, timing optimization and effectiveness observations remain local and are not part of full record backups.

5. Optional Apple Health

After separate iPhone authorization, the app coordinates its own plain-water Health samples, including water received from Watch or other synced devices. Tea, coffee, milk, juice and other drinks are not written to Health. Health data is not sold or used for ads or profiling.

Water sync reads only information needed to coordinate the app’s own water samples; Health is not a full backup of all drink types. Disabling sync does not automatically delete existing Health samples. Deleting app entries attempts to delete corresponding app-owned samples when authorized; failures can be retried. Mac does not directly access Health.

You can separately authorize read access to stand hours and steps for the weekly activity recap and optional Health-assisted reminders. Heleme does not write activity data to Apple Health. Daily summaries stay on this iPhone and are excluded from Heleme’s iCloud sync, Watch sync and backup files. Select Stop Using Activity Data under Settings → Apple Health to clear the local summaries. Movement you log manually is a separate app record.

6. Optional iCloud and Watch sync

Enabling cross-device sync on iPhone or Mac stores drink records, goal history, merge metadata, goals, cups, reminder preferences, usual and favorite drinks, syncable companion preferences, medal acknowledgements, recap start and manually logged movement in your Apple Account’s private CloudKit database. The developer does not receive these through its own server or sell them. Health database samples themselves are not uploaded as an app document.

Watch exchanges records, required settings and recent goal history through its paired iPhone, rather than acting as an independent CloudKit client. Offline entries remain pending on Watch until acknowledged; verify sync before erasing it. iCloud depends on the same Apple Account, network and system opportunities. Account changes pause sync; disabling sync does not delete local or existing cloud data.

Legacy settings backups are different from full record sync and do not include all records. A device that has not successfully completed the new sync can check or restore legacy backups. After success that area is hidden without deleting old cloud data. Manage cloud data through system iCloud storage settings; disable sync on relevant devices and keep another backup before deletion.

7. Recovery, files and deletion

A local recovery copy is not an off-device backup and may not survive uninstalling, erasing or losing a device. Unreadable original data is preserved rather than replaced with empty records. Recovery can omit changes made after the copy.

Full JSON backups include drink records, goal history, settings, companion preferences, movement and merge metadata. iPhone files also include visual style, appearance mode, droplet speech and drinking-sound switches. Imports are previewed before confirmation, merge identifiers/revisions and apply backed-up settings. CSV is a report, not a restorable full backup. Exported files have no extra app-managed password or encryption; the system and your selected services handle saving and sharing. Deleting app records does not delete exported files. The last successful export time is local only; it stores no selected file location and does not prove the file still exists.

You can revoke permissions or disable sync and reminders while retaining local recording. Deletion state can sync, but does not immediately physically erase every backup. Uninstalling does not guarantee removal from Health, iCloud, other devices or exports. Verify data on a replacement device before erasing the old one.

8. Widgets, third parties and contact

App Group snapshots support widget display and quick recording, including totals, goals, cups, drink types, favorites, language, themes and update times. Lock Screen, desktop or watch-face displays can expose records to others. The system controls refresh timing.

Linked reference sites handle their own requests. This site embeds no tracking or analytics scripts; GitHub and Apple services may process requests under their own policies. Support messages are handled by your email service and the support mailbox. Redact screenshots and do not send passwords or unnecessary full records. Contact support@fenrir-inc.com.cn for privacy questions.

9. Use and limitations

Heleme is a habit-recording tool, not diagnosis, treatment or individualized medical advice. Goals, reference amounts and reminders are not medical safety limits. Children and people with fluid restrictions should follow guardian or professional guidance. Do not drink large amounts at once to complete a ring. Notifications are not medical alarms requiring punctual delivery.

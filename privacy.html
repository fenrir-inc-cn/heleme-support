隐私政策

1. 适用范围与数据

本政策适用于 Fenrir Chengdu Inc. 的喝了么（Heleme）iPhone、Apple Watch、Mac App 与配套小组件。功能以已安装版本及平台为准；Mac 的商店供应情况以实际商店页面为准。无需注册，不接入广告、第三方追踪或行为分析，不运行接收记录的开发者服务器。App 不申请姓名、通讯录、位置或照片权限，也不使用拍照识别或外部 AI 分析记录。

App 在本机保存你主动记录的饮品种类、容量、发生时间、来源及编辑状态，以及目标、四个快捷杯量与常用杯量选择、常用饮品、收藏、主题、反馈和提醒偏好。也可记录你手动确认的起身活动时间；不会读取运动传感器来判定是否起身。编号、修改版本、删除标记和已处理事件用于去重与合并。

2. 设备同步与通知

配对 Watch 通过 Apple Watch Connectivity 与 iPhone 交换所需记录、收藏和设置。离线事件保留在 Watch，等待手机确认；卸载前尚未同步的记录可能丢失。Watch 通过配对手机参与 iCloud 同步，并非独立 CloudKit 客户端。

通知在本机安排，需要系统权限；起身提醒可在设置中关闭。提醒和暂停状态有设备本地部分，多个设备不保证自动避免重复通知。系统权限、专注模式、通知摘要、后台机会和队列容量影响实际送达。App 不运行自有推送服务器。

3. 智能提醒、成长和洞察

洞察、奖章和每周回顾在设备上根据记录计算，不是健康评分。近两周洞察查看今天之前的 14 天；缺少记录不代表没有喝水。奖章可随记录更正而更新，获得日期依据记录推算。

提醒账本、可核实的历史目标、时段优化与效果观察保存在本机，不包含在 App 的跨设备 iCloud 文档、Watch 传输或 JSON 记录备份中。优化需确认，可从今天的下次提醒菜单或成长中的洞察管理；固定模式下保留但不参与安排。系统设备备份可能另行保留本机信息。

4. 可选 Apple 健康

在 iPhone 主动开启并授权后，App 只协调自己写入 Apple 健康的白水记录，包含从 Watch 或其他同步设备收到的白水记录。茶、咖啡、牛奶、果汁和其他饮品不写入 Apple 健康；它们的原始容量和发生时间仍保存在 App，并在开启新版 iCloud 跨设备同步后参与同步和换机恢复。记录容量不是身体吸收水分的估计，不做吸收率换算。App 不申请读取其他 App 的饮水记录或无关健康指标。

App 查询自己写入的样本以处理去重、修改、删除和经确认的恢复。关闭不会自动删除已有健康样本。Apple 健康的系统云同步由 Apple 独立管理，Mac 不直接读写健康样本。

5. 可选 iCloud 跨设备同步与旧备份

3.0 的跨设备同步为可选功能。开启后，饮品记录及合并所需的编号、修改和删除状态，目标、杯量、提醒偏好、常用饮品、收藏、主题、简洁反馈、奖章确认状态、回顾起点及起身设置与活动事件等，存储在你 Apple 账户的 CloudKit 私有数据库，用于 iPhone 与 Mac 同步。健康数据库本身的样本不放入该文档。开发者不通过自有服务器接收或出售这些记录。

同步取决于登录账户、网络及 App 活跃状态，不承诺即时或所有设备挂起时持续更新。切换 Apple 账户时同步会暂停，避免把原账户的本机记录自动上传到新账户。关闭同步不删除本机或已有云端数据。请在系统 iCloud 存储管理中管理云端数据，移除前先关闭相关设备同步并另存备份。

2.x 的旧版 iCloud 设置备份只包含偏好。3.0 不再自动写入旧设置备份；尚未成功完成新版同步时，统一的 iCloud 页面保留旧备份检查和恢复入口。设备成功完成过新版同步后，该旧版区域隐藏，即使后来关闭新版同步也不会重新出现；隐藏不会删除旧云端备份。更早版本的旧记录备份如存在，可在该区域单独检查。旧设置备份不等于新的跨设备记录同步。

6. 本机恢复副本与手动文件

本机恢复副本用于主数据无法读取时恢复，不能代替设备外备份，也不保证卸载、抹掉或丢失设备后可用。更新失败可重试；恢复可能缺少副本时间之后的变化。无法读取的原始数据保留本机以供恢复。

完整 JSON 备份包含饮品记录、目标与提醒设置、3.0 收藏与伴随偏好、活动记录和合并所需元数据。导入先预览再确认，按标识与版本合并并应用备份设置。CSV 为包含饮品种类的报表，不能用作完整备份恢复。JSON/CSV 不设额外文件密码或 App 加密，保存与分享由系统文件界面及所选服务处理；删除 App 内记录不会删除已导出的文件。

最近成功导出时间仅保存在本机，不保存选中文件位置，也不代表文件此后仍存在。换机前请确认新设备已有所需记录，并保留可信的独立文件备份。

7. 小组件与显示

App 与本机组件通过受控共享空间保存显示所需快照：今日总量、目标、杯量与常用选择、饮品、收藏、语言、主题、时区和更新时间等。iPhone/Mac 桌面组件可发起记录，Mac 可能需要唤起 App；锁屏组件显示进度并打开 App。Watch 表盘组件使用配套本机数据；系统控制刷新时机，快照时间不证明跨设备传输成功。

语言跟随平台选择，外观用于显示；主题和简洁反馈等伴随偏好可随已开启的 iCloud 同步。建议出处按 App 语言选择，不读取位置。锁屏、桌面或表盘可能向旁人显示记录，请按需要移除组件或调整系统隐私设置。

8. 选择、删除与保留

可撤销通知和健康权限、关闭起身提醒、健康同步或跨设备同步，继续使用本机记录。历史页可以删除记录；Mac 也提供右键操作。开启 iCloud 时删除状态参与合并，但并非立即从所有备份中物理清除。健康同步有权限时尝试删除对应自有样本，失败保留待处理状态。

卸载不保证同时删除 Apple 健康、iCloud、另一设备或导出文件中的数据。系统设备备份按 Apple 设置处理。更改账户或抹掉旧设备前，请核实恢复情况。

9. 第三方与联系

出处链接由相应网站处理。本站不嵌入追踪或分析脚本；托管方 GitHub 可能按其政策处理访问请求。Apple 服务按 Apple 的隐私政策处理。联系支持时，邮件内容由你的邮件服务与支持邮箱处理；请遮挡截图敏感信息，不要发送密码或不必要的完整记录。

我们不出售数据或用于广告、画像。处理方式变化时更新本政策，并在需要时取得许可。隐私问题请联系 support@fenrir-inc.com.cn。

10. 使用说明

本 App 是习惯记录工具，不提供诊断、治疗或个体化医疗建议。目标、参考量和提醒不是医学安全线；儿童及需限制饮水者应遵循监护人或专业医护建议，不为完成圆环集中大量饮水。通知不能作为必须准时送达的医疗提醒。

Privacy policy

1. Scope and data

This policy covers Heleme by Fenrir Chengdu Inc., its iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac apps and widgets. Features depend on installed version and platform; Mac availability follows its actual store listing. No registration, advertising, third-party tracking or behavioral analytics is used. We do not run a developer server receiving your records, request contacts, location or photo access, or identify drinks through photos or external AI.

Local data includes the drink types, volumes, drinking times, sources and edits you enter; goals, four quick cup sizes and the usual cup selection, usual drinks, favorites, themes, feedback and reminder preferences; and movement breaks you manually confirm. No motion sensor determines whether you stood up. Identifiers, revisions, deletion markers and handled-event IDs support merging and deduplication.

2. Devices and notifications

A paired Watch exchanges necessary records, favorites and settings with iPhone using Apple Watch Connectivity. Offline events wait for phone acknowledgement and can be lost if the Watch app is removed before transfer. Watch participates in cloud sync through iPhone, not as a standalone CloudKit client.

Notifications are scheduled locally with permission. Movement reminders are optional. Some delivery and pause settings are device-local; automatic duplicate-notification suppression across devices is not promised. Focus, summaries, permissions, queue limits and system background opportunities affect delivery. No developer push server is operated.

3. Smart reminders, growth and insights

Insights, medals and weekly recaps are calculated on device from records, not health scores. Two-week insights use the 14 days before today; missing entries do not mean no drinking. Correcting records can change medals and their record-derived dates.

Reminder ledgers, verifiable historical goals, saved period optimization and effect observations stay local and are not included in the app cloud document, Watch transfer or full JSON record backup. Confirm and manage optimization through the next-reminder menu or Growth insights. Fixed mode retains it without applying it. Apple system device backups may preserve local information separately.

4. Optional Apple Health

On iPhone, only after enabling and granting permission, the app coordinates the plain-water records it writes to Apple Health, including water received from Watch or another synced device. Tea, coffee, milk, juice and other drinks are not written to Apple Health. Their original volumes and times remain in the app and participate in cross-device sync and device restoration when the new iCloud sync is enabled. Logged volume is not an estimate of absorbed water; no absorption conversion is applied. Access to other apps’ water or unrelated health metrics is not requested.

The app queries its own samples for deduplication, edits, deletion and confirmed restoration. Switching off does not remove existing Health samples. Apple manages system Health cloud sync separately; the Mac app does not directly read or write Health samples.

5. Optional iCloud sync and legacy backups

In 3.0, cross-device sync is optional. Enabling it stores drink records with identifiers, revisions and deletion state, goals, cups, reminder preferences, usual drinks, favorites, themes, simple feedback, medal acknowledgements, recap start, movement settings and manually logged movement events in your Apple account’s private CloudKit database for iPhone/Mac synchronization. The Health database’s samples themselves are not placed in this document. The developer does not receive these records through an app server or sell them.

Sync depends on account, network and app activity; immediate updates or continuous delivery while all apps are suspended are not promised. Changing Apple Accounts pauses sync to avoid uploading the preceding account’s local records automatically. Disabling sync does not delete local or cloud data. Manage cloud storage in system iCloud settings; first disable sync on relevant devices and keep an independent backup.

The 2.x legacy iCloud backup contains preferences only. Version 3.0 no longer automatically writes the old backup. The unified iCloud page retains legacy check/restore actions until this device has successfully completed the new sync. After that success, the legacy area stays hidden even if sync is later disabled; hiding it does not delete old cloud backups. Any older record backup can be checked separately in that area. Legacy preferences backup is distinct from the new record sync.

6. Local recovery and files

The local recovery copy helps when primary storage cannot be read. It is not an off-device backup and may not survive uninstalling, erasing or losing a device. Failed updates can be retried; recovery can omit changes after the copy’s date. Unreadable original data remains local for recovery.

A full JSON backup includes drink records, goal/reminder settings, 3.0 favorites and companion preferences, activity events and merge metadata. Imports show a preview before confirmation, merge by identifier/revision and apply backed-up settings. CSV is a report including drink types, not a restorable full backup. JSON/CSV have no extra app-managed password or encryption. The system file/share interface and your selected service handle saving and sharing; deleting app records does not delete exported copies.

The last successful export time stays local without storing the selected file location. It does not prove the file still exists. Before replacing devices, verify the needed records on the new device and keep a trusted independent backup.

7. Widgets and display

The app and its components use controlled local shared storage for snapshots such as today’s total, goal, cup, drink, favorites, language, theme, time zone and update time. iPhone/Mac desktop widgets can initiate recording; Mac may open the app. Lock Screen widgets show progress and open the app. Watch complications use companion data. The system controls refresh timing; a snapshot timestamp does not prove cross-device delivery.

Language follows platform choices. Companion preferences such as theme and simple feedback may sync when iCloud sync is enabled. Advice sources follow app language without accessing location. Widgets or watch faces may reveal records to others; remove them or adjust system privacy settings if needed.

8. Choices, deletion and retention

You can revoke notification/Health permissions or turn off movement reminders, Health sync or iCloud sync while continuing local recording. Delete records from History; Mac also offers context-menu actions. With iCloud enabled, deletion state participates in merging but does not immediately physically erase all backup copies. Authorized Health sync attempts to delete corresponding app-owned samples and retains pending work after failures.

Uninstalling does not guarantee deletion from Health, iCloud, other devices or exported files. Apple system backups follow your system settings. Verify restoration before changing accounts or erasing an old device.

9. Third parties and contact

Source links are handled by their websites. This site embeds no tracking/analytics scripts; GitHub hosting may process access requests under its policy. Apple services follow Apple’s policies. Support email is processed by your mail provider and our support mailbox. Redact screenshots and never send passwords or unnecessary complete records.

We do not sell data or use it for advertising or profiling. We update this policy and obtain permission when required if practices change. For privacy questions contact support@fenrir-inc.com.cn.

10. Use and limitations

This is a habit-recording tool, not diagnosis, treatment or personalized medical advice. Goals, references and reminders are not medical safety limits. Children and people with fluid restrictions should follow guardian or professional guidance. Do not drink excessively to complete a ring or rely on notifications as time-critical medical reminders.

3.1 帮助与指南

1. 今天：饮品与撤销

首页六种图标可切换白水、茶、咖啡、牛奶、果汁和其他饮品，主按钮按当前饮品与杯量记录。更改常用设定只修改常用饮品和杯量，保存会覆盖现有设定，不会新增饮水记录；也可在设置 → 饮水目标与杯量更改。记录按实际饮用容量计入进度，不做吸收率换算。记录后可撤销，历史支持补记与修改。

2. 成长与起身活动

成长包含我的奖章、每周回顾和近两周饮水习惯洞察。奖章提示可点入查看获得日期；日期由记录推算，不鼓励超量饮水。设置 → 可选提醒可开启或关闭起身活动，手动点完成会记录一次活动；它不是自动站立检测。

3. 提醒、优化和明天的预览

设置 → 提醒计划选择固定或智能提醒、时段与每日上限；起止不能相同，支持跨午夜。智能提醒可按已记录习惯调整，基础间隔最多缩短 15%。成长 → 饮水习惯洞察或今天下次提醒的菜单可查看、应用或关闭可用优化。优化先调整一条最多 15 分钟，必要时每日最多增加一次且仍受每日上限限制。固定模式不应用优化。

预览展示下一次能实际改变的提醒；如果今天对应时段已过，显示明天或之后的日期是正常的。洞察查看今天之前的 14 天，不代表健康结论。暂停、达标停止、时段结束或每日额度都可能使下次提醒落到明天；请同时阅读首页原因。

4. 通知不响或排程失败

检查系统通知权限、专注模式、App 提醒开关、暂停状态及允许时段。帮助中的通知检查可发送测试；排程失败后在首页菜单或提醒设置点刷新。系统决定最终送达与手机/Watch 路由；App 只续排有限时段，长期不打开 App 无法保证持续或精确提醒。

5. iCloud、Apple 健康与文件备份

设置 → 跨设备同步是 3.0 的统一 iCloud 页面。iPhone 与 Mac 使用相同 Apple 账户，分别开启同步后合并记录与偏好；Watch 通过配对 iPhone 同步。白水以外五类饮品的原始容量、时间和种类同样参与新版同步，无需通过 Apple 健康恢复。旧设置备份不包含 3.0 的全部记录，仅在本机尚未成功完成新版同步时显示检查/恢复区域；成功后隐藏，不删除旧云端备份。切换账户后同步暂停，先确认使用原账户。

Apple 健康只在 iPhone 单独授权，并仅处理白水条目；其他饮品由 App 与可选的新版 iCloud 同步保存。换机前在旧设备确认新版同步成功；新设备使用相同 Apple 账户开启同步，再核对各类饮品与设定后抹掉旧设备。Apple 健康不是完整 App 备份。数据备份与恢复还可导出独立 JSON 文件，必要时在新设备预览、导入并确认；CSV 仅为报表。导入上限 20 MB、合并后最多 100000 条。删除或抹掉旧设备前先在新设备核实记录，勿仅凭更新时间判断同步成功。

6. 历史与大字体

历史可切换趋势/日历，查看最近、周、月、年，点击图表查看数值，点查看记录进入明细。小屏/大字体页可滚动；滚动布局使用轻点查看图表，避免与上下滑动冲突。日历可补记、编辑和删除，补记与修改均可选择带图标的饮品种类、容量及实际时间；Mac 提供右键操作。缺少记录不等于没有喝水。

7. Watch 与小组件

在 iPhone Watch App 的我的手表中安装喝了么，App 内 Apple Watch 帮助页提供安装与表盘指南及 Apple 官方链接。离线记录等待手机确认，清理手表前确认没有待同步项。iPhone/Mac 桌面组件支持常用杯量、预设量或收藏饮品快捷记录；Mac 可能唤起 App。锁屏和表盘的能力取决于系统支持，刷新时机由系统控制。

8. Mac 使用与提醒

Mac 版可独立记录，不要求 iPhone 正在运行。功能是否已可下载请以 Mac App Store 为准。设置可管理本机通知、菜单栏、登录启动、目标、计划、同步和备份；Mac 饮水提醒初始关闭，可按需开启，避免与手机重复。Mac 不直接接入 Apple 健康。

9. 语言、外观与反馈

App 语言跟随系统的 App 语言设置；iPhone 可由外观与语言页面打开系统设置。简洁反馈保留记录、进度和提醒，减少装饰动画；系统减少动态效果同样受到尊重。升级到 3.0 的介绍可跳过，不强制开启新增提醒。

10. 本机恢复与联系

本机恢复副本不是设备外备份；失败可重试并检查副本日期。联系 support@fenrir-inc.com.cn 时提供平台、App/系统版本及复现步骤，遮挡敏感信息，不发送 Apple 账户密码。

容量如何快速输入

在更改常用设定、饮水目标、补记或修改记录页面，直接点按减号与加号中间的数字输入，无需另找输入框。杯量快捷区最多四项；自定义容量时替换 400 mL 选项。目标可在 500–3,000 mL、常用杯量可在 50–1,000 mL、单次新记录可在 1–1,000 mL 范围内设置。这些是 App 输入限制，不是医学安全界线，也不是推荐饮用量。

保存后出现健康同步提示

记录首先保存在喝了么；“等待同步”或“正在同步”不表示保存失败。只有实际同步失败才显示“待重试”和重试操作，未解决状态不会按保存成功提示的时间自动消失。关闭健康同步或记录非白水饮品时，不会尝试向 Apple 健康写入该条饮品。可在 Apple 健康设置页检查授权与状态。

3.1 help and guides

1. Today: drinks and undo

Today’s six icons switch between water, tea, coffee, milk, juice and other drinks. The main button records the current drink and amount. Change usual settings changes only your usual drink and cup: saving replaces those preferences without adding a record. You can also change them in Settings → Water goal and cup size. Progress uses actual logged volume without an absorption conversion. Undo a recent entry or edit/backfill in History.

2. Growth and movement

Growth contains My medals, Weekly recap and two-week insights. Open a medal notice to see its record-derived earned date; medals do not encourage excess intake. Enable or disable movement reminders in Settings → Optional reminders. Manually completing a break records an event, not automatic standing detection.

3. Reminders, optimization and tomorrow’s preview

Settings → Reminder schedule offers fixed/smart mode, hours and daily limits. Start/end must differ; overnight windows are supported. Smart reminders can shorten the base interval by at most 15%. Manage available optimization from Growth insights or Today’s next-reminder menu. It first shifts one reminder by at most 15 minutes; if needed at most one extra daily reminder shares the daily limit. Fixed mode does not apply optimization.

The preview shows the next reminder that can actually change. It can fall tomorrow or later when today’s relevant period has passed. Insights use the 14 days before today, not health conclusions. Pauses, goal-based stopping, quiet hours or the daily allowance may also move the next reminder to tomorrow; read Today’s accompanying reason.

4. Missing notifications or scheduling failures

Check system permissions, Focus, app reminders, pauses and allowed hours. Help offers a test reminder; after scheduling errors, use Refresh reminders in Today’s menu or reminder settings. The system controls delivery and phone/Watch routing. Scheduling covers a finite horizon; indefinite unattended or exact delivery is not guaranteed.

5. iCloud, Health and files

Settings → Cross-device sync is the unified 3.0 iCloud page. Enable sync separately on iPhone and Mac using the same Apple Account to merge records and preferences. Watch uses its paired iPhone. The original volumes, times and types of all five non-water drinks participate too, independently of Apple Health. Old settings backups do not contain all 3.0 records. Their recovery area appears only until this device successfully completes the new sync; it is then hidden without deleting the old cloud backup. An account change pauses sync; verify the original account.

Apple Health is separately authorized on iPhone for plain-water entries only. Other drinks are retained by the app and optional new iCloud sync. Before replacing a device, confirm successful sync on the old device, enable sync with the same Apple Account on the new one, and verify every drink type and your preferences before erasing the old device. Health is not a full app backup. Data backup & restore also exports an independent JSON file, which you can preview, import and confirm when needed. CSV is only a report. Imports are limited to 20 MB and a merged maximum of 100,000 records. Verify records on the new device before erasing the old one; an update timestamp alone does not prove successful transfer.

6. History and larger text

Use Trends/Calendar for recent, week, month and year views. Tap a chart to inspect, then View records for details. Small-screen/larger-text pages scroll; scrolling layouts use chart taps to avoid vertical-pan conflicts. Add, edit or delete from Calendar. Backfill and correction both offer drink types with icons, amount and actual time; Mac also offers context menus. Missing records do not mean no drinking.

7. Watch and widgets

Install Heleme under My Watch in the iPhone Watch app. In-app Apple Watch help provides installation/watch-face guides and official Apple links. Offline entries wait for acknowledgement; check pending data before clearing Watch. iPhone/Mac desktop widgets offer usual cups, presets or favorite drinks; Mac may open the app. Lock Screen/complication features and refresh timing depend on the system.

8. Mac

The native Mac app records independently without a running iPhone. Download availability follows the Mac App Store listing. Settings covers local notifications, menu bar, login launch, goals, schedules, sync and backups. Mac drinking reminders start off; enable them where useful to avoid duplicate device alerts. Mac does not directly access Apple Health.

9. Language, appearance and feedback

App language follows platform app-language settings; iPhone Appearance & language can open system settings. Simple feedback preserves records, progress and reminders while reducing decorative animation. System Reduce Motion is respected. Existing users can dismiss the 3.0 introduction without enabling new reminders.

10. Recovery and support

A local recovery copy is not an off-device backup. Retry failures and check the copy date. Email support@fenrir-inc.com.cn with platform, app/system versions and reproduction steps. Redact sensitive screenshots and never send Apple Account passwords.

Entering an amount

In Change usual settings, goal settings and record backfill/edit, tap the number between minus and plus to type directly. Cup shortcuts show at most four choices; a custom amount replaces 400 mL. Goals accept 500–3,000 mL, usual cups 50–1,000 mL and new entries 1–1,000 mL. These are app input limits, not medical safety thresholds or recommended amounts.

Health status after saving

A record is saved in Heleme first. Waiting or syncing does not mean the save failed. Only an actual sync failure shows a retry action, and unresolved sync status does not expire with the saved-record acknowledgement. With Health sync off, or for a non-water drink, the app does not attempt to write that entry to Health. Check permissions and status in Apple Health settings in the app.

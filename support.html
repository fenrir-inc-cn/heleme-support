常见问题

没有收到提醒怎么办？

先检查 App 设置中已开启提醒，iOS 允许通知，且当前处于提醒时段、当天未达记录目标。用“发送测试提醒”锁屏验证。专注模式、通知摘要和手表路由可能影响显示位置与提示方式。

长时间不打开 App，还会提醒吗？

App 会提前安排有限的一段时间，并在使用时续排，同时在系统允许时尝试后台续排。若需你操作，会在计划结束前发送续排提示；打开 App 或选择“继续提醒”即可重新安排。后台刷新和通知送达由 iOS 管理，无法承诺永久无人操作或每次准时提示。

手表上找不到 App？

在 iPhone 的 Watch App 中查看可用 App 并安装。手机与手表须配对。手表上的临时断连记录会在连接恢复后尝试补传，请在清除手表前确认待同步数量归零。

如何修改或删除记录？

在历史页选择日期，点按明细修改饮水量，左滑删除。首页记录后可撤销。开启健康同步时，App 会同步更新自己写入的健康条目；锁屏、权限或系统错误可能使同步待完成，解锁后在设置中重试。

Apple 健康与 iCloud 有什么区别？

Apple 健康可选保存本 App 的饮水记录；App 自己的 iCloud 只备份目标、杯量和提醒设置。新手机需重新授权健康，并单独选择恢复本 App 的健康记录。系统健康云同步需在 Apple 设置中自行管理。

饮水目标适合所有人吗？

不是。1500／1700 mL 是饮水参考选项，不计食物中的水分，不构成个体化医疗建议。可在设置中调整；不要为达标集中饮水。有饮水限制时遵循医嘱。

联系时提供什么？

请说明问题、App 版本、iOS／watchOS 版本及复现步骤。可附必要截图，但请先遮挡个人或健康信息；无需提供 Apple 账户密码或完整饮水记录。

Help and troubleshooting

No reminder?

Check app and system notification permissions, reminder hours and whether today's recording goal is complete. Try the test reminder while locked. Focus, summaries and Watch routing can affect where and how an alert appears.

What if I stop opening the app?

The app schedules a finite period in advance and renews it when used, with background renewal when iOS permits. A renewal notice asks you to open the app or choose Continue reminders before the schedule ends. iOS controls background opportunities and delivery; unattended indefinite or exact-time alerts cannot be guaranteed.

Watch installation and offline records

Look for Heleme under available apps in the iPhone Watch app and install it on your paired watch. Offline records wait for transfer; confirm that no records are pending before clearing the watch.

Edit, delete and Health sync

Select a date in History, tap an entry to edit or swipe to delete. The latest home entry can be undone. With Health sync enabled, the app updates only its own Health entries. Unlock iPhone and retry in Settings if synchronization is pending.

Health versus iCloud

Apple Health optionally stores water records. App iCloud backup stores preferences only. On a new phone, reauthorize Health and explicitly restore this app's records; manage system Health cloud sync separately in Apple's settings.

Reference goals

1500/1700 mL are adjustable drinking-reference options, excluding food water, not personalized medical advice. Do not drink excessively to reach a target. Follow medical fluid restrictions where applicable.

Contacting support

Email the issue, app and OS versions, and steps to reproduce. Redact personal and health information from screenshots. Never send passwords or unnecessary water history.

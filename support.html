常见问题与使用指南

以下设置路径对应 1.1 版本的界面，部分功能和入口随安装版本而异；较早版本的同类功能可能直接列在设置首页。

如何调整目标和常用杯量？

打开“设置 → 饮水与提醒 → 饮水目标与杯量”，调整每日饮水记录目标、参考选项和常用杯量。“如何选择目标”在单独的分组中，提供说明和来源。1500／1700 mL 是可调整的参考选项，不计食物中的水分，不是针对个人的医疗建议。不要为达标集中饮水；有饮水限制时请遵循医嘱。

如何切换语言或深色模式？

在“设置 → 语言 → App 语言”中选择语言。“跟随系统”使用 iOS 为本 App 选择的语言，也可通过“打开 iOS App 设置”调整。外观分组位于语言下方，可选择“跟随系统”“浅色”或“深色”。界面选择会保存在本机。

为什么完成目标后，下次提醒是明天？

当天记录达到目标后，今天不再安排喝水提醒，通常从次日设定的开始时间恢复。首页会显示达标说明及实际下一次提醒时间。尚未达标时，提醒按设定间隔及实际喝水时间重新计时；提醒时段结束后也会顺延至次日。请勿为了提醒或进度环而额外饮水。

没有收到提醒怎么办？

打开“设置 → 饮水与提醒 → 提醒计划”，检查提醒开关、时段和通知状态，再确认 iOS 允许通知、当前处于提醒时段且当天尚未达标。也请检查首页是否暂停了提醒。“设置 → 帮助与关于 → 使用指南 → 检查通知”提供排查说明和测试入口。专注模式、通知摘要及手机／手表路由会影响显示位置与提示方式，测试通知不能保证所有正式提醒都能准时送达。

长时间不打开 App，还会提醒吗？

App 提前安排有限的一段时间，并在使用时续排，同时在系统允许时尝试后台续排。若需要你操作，会在计划结束前发送续排提示；打开 App、选择“继续提醒”或使用“刷新提醒”可重新安排。后台刷新和通知送达由 iOS 管理，无法保证永久无人操作或每次准时提示。

如何修改、补记、删除或导出记录？

在“历史”切换趋势或月历；选择日期后查看明细，可点按记录修改、左滑删除，并使用添加入口补记饮水。首页刚记录的饮水可在短暂出现的提示中撤销。历史页的导出入口生成 CSV 报表；需要换机恢复时，请使用下面的完整 JSON 备份。

Apple 健康、iCloud 和完整备份有什么区别？

  • Apple 健康：可选同步本 App 的饮水记录。设置入口为“同步与设备 → Apple 健康”，可查看状态、最近成功时间并手动同步；恢复健康记录需要主动确认。
  • App 内的 iCloud 备份：只备份目标、杯量和提醒设置，不包含饮水记录。入口为“同步与设备 → iCloud 备份”。“把设置备份到 iCloud”不会创建完整饮水记录备份。
  • 完整文件备份：在“设置 → 帮助与关于 → 数据与隐私 → 数据与备份”导出或导入 JSON 文件，包含饮水记录与目标、杯量、提醒设置。

系统健康云同步与系统设备备份由 Apple 的设置管理，与以上 App 功能分开。语言和外观选择不包含在 App 内的 iCloud 设置备份或完整记录备份中。

如何导入完整备份？

在“数据与备份”选择“导入备份”，选取本 App 导出的 JSON 文件，查看新增、更新、删除及设置变化的预览，确认后再导入。记录按编号和版本合并，并保留删除状态，避免重复计数或恢复已经删除的记录；备份中的目标、杯量和提醒设置会替换对应的当前设置。

单个文件上限为 20 MB，合并后最多保留 100,000 条记录，合并数据也需符合大小限制。无法读取、格式不兼容或超过限制时，App 会拒绝导入并保留本机数据。CSV 报表不能作为完整备份导入。

换机、卸载或抹掉设备前应做什么？

先导出完整 JSON 备份，保存在你信任的位置，并在新设备确认需要的记录已恢复。若使用 Apple 健康，也请检查 App 的健康同步状态和系统的健康备份／同步设置。导出文件包含饮水历史，App 不另设文件密码，请妥善保管；保存到 iCloud Drive 或其他文件服务取决于你在系统文件界面的选择。

“最近的本机副本”是什么？更新失败怎么办？

它是保存在这台 iPhone 上、用于主数据无法读取时的恢复副本，不代替设备外的备份。恢复前请核对副本日期，较新的修改可能不在其中。出现副本更新失败提示时，已有主记录仍可使用，可通过“重试本机副本”重试；如主数据已无法读取，请使用界面提供的恢复或备份导入操作。

手表上找不到 App，或表盘没有更新？

打开“设置 → 同步与设备 → Apple Watch”，查看安装到手表、添加到表盘的指南、连接状态和最近收到的数据。安装需在 iPhone 的 Watch App 中完成，手机与手表须配对。可手动刷新连接；显示已请求检查不代表已经完成传输。临时断连时，记录会留在手表等待补传，请在清除手表前确认待同步数量归零。表盘和小组件的刷新时机由系统决定。

如何添加桌面或锁屏小组件？

在“设置 → 帮助与关于 → 使用指南”查看对应的添加指南。桌面小组件可显示进度并快捷记水；锁屏小组件显示进度，点按打开 App。先完成首次引导；更新后若组件列表未出现 App，请先打开 App 一次。添加、移除和锁屏隐私设置由系统界面管理。

可以重新查看或退出引导吗？

在“设置 → 帮助与关于 → 使用指南 → 再看一遍引导”重新打开引导，每一页都可关闭并返回帮助页。首次引导不显示关闭按钮；在系统健康或通知授权界面中，你仍可自行决定是否允许，拒绝授权不影响本机记水。

健康同步一直未完成怎么办？

确认系统健康权限及 App 内同步开关，解锁 iPhone 后在 Apple 健康设置页重试。App 只协调自己写入的健康条目；最近成功时间只代表一次完整同步成功，不代表另一台设备已经收到系统健康数据。关闭同步不会自动删除已经写入健康的记录。

联系支持时提供什么？

在“设置 → 帮助与关于 → 联系与支持 → 通过邮件联系我们”打开邮件，或直接写信至 support@fenrir-inc.com.cn。请说明问题、App 版本、iOS／watchOS 版本和复现步骤。截图请先遮挡个人或健康信息；无需提供 Apple 账户密码或完整饮水记录。

Help and guides

The settings paths below describe version 1.1. Availability and labels depend on your installed version; earlier versions may list equivalent controls directly on the main Settings screen.

Change the goal and usual cup size

Open Settings → Daily routine → Water goal and cup size. Adjust the daily drinking target, reference choice and usual cup size. Choosing a target is in a separate group with explanations and sources. The 1500/1700 mL choices are adjustable references excluding food water, not personalized medical advice. Do not drink excessively to reach a target; follow any medical fluid restrictions.

Choose a language or Dark appearance

Open Settings → Language → App language. Follow System uses the app language chosen in iOS; Open iOS app settings lets you adjust it there. Appearance is below Language, with Follow System, Light and Dark choices. Your interface choices are saved locally.

Why is the next reminder tomorrow after reaching the goal?

Once today's recorded amount reaches the goal, no further drinking reminders are scheduled for that day. They normally resume at your configured start time the next day. Today shows a completion note and the actual next reminder time. Before the goal is reached, reminders follow your interval and restart from the actual drink time; the end of reminder hours also moves the next reminder to the following day. Do not drink extra for reminders or the progress ring.

No reminder?

Open Settings → Daily routine → Reminder schedule to check the switch, hours and notification status. Confirm iOS permits notifications, you are within reminder hours and today's goal is not complete. Check whether reminders are paused on Today. Settings → Help & about → Usage guides → Check notifications provides troubleshooting and a test reminder. Focus, summaries and phone/Watch routing affect delivery. A successful test does not guarantee every scheduled reminder will arrive on time.

What if I stop opening the app?

The app schedules a finite period in advance and renews it when used, with background renewal when iOS permits. A renewal notice asks you to open the app or choose Continue reminders before the schedule ends. Refresh reminders can also renew the schedule. iOS controls background opportunities and delivery; unattended indefinite or exact-time alerts cannot be guaranteed.

Edit, add, delete or export water records

In History, switch between Trends and Calendar and select a date to view entries. Tap an entry to edit it, swipe to delete, or use the add control to enter a drink. A briefly shown confirmation lets you undo a new home entry. History exports CSV reports; use the full JSON backup below for restoring records on another device.

Apple Health, iCloud and full backups

  • Apple Health: optionally syncs this app's water records. Under Sync & devices → Apple Health, check status, the last successful sync time or sync manually. Restoring Health records requires confirmation.
  • In-app iCloud backup: stores goal, cup and reminder preferences only, without water records. Find it under Sync & devices → iCloud backup. Back up settings to iCloud does not create a full record backup.
  • Full file backup: export or import a JSON file under Settings → Help & about → Data & privacy → Data & backups. It contains water records and goal, cup and reminder settings.

System Health cloud sync and system device backups are managed by Apple separately. Language and appearance choices are not included in the app's iCloud preferences backup or full record backup.

Import a full backup

Choose Import backup under Data & backups and select a JSON file exported by Heleme. Review the changes to records and settings before confirming. Records merge by identifier and version while preserving deletion state to avoid duplicates or restoring deleted entries. Imported goal, cup and reminder settings replace their current counterparts.

A file can be up to 20 MB; the merged data must also fit the size limit and contain at most 100,000 records. Unreadable, incompatible or over-limit imports are rejected while local data is retained. A CSV report cannot be imported as a full backup.

Before replacing, erasing or uninstalling

Export a full JSON backup to a trusted location and confirm the records you need have been restored on the new device. If you use Apple Health, also check app sync and the system's Health backup/sync settings. Exported files contain water history and have no extra app-managed password. Saving to iCloud Drive or another file service depends on your choice in the system file interface.

What is the latest local copy? What if it cannot update?

This recovery copy stays on the current iPhone and helps when primary data cannot be read; it does not replace a backup outside the device. Check its date before restoring, as later changes may be missing. If only the copy failed to update, saved primary records remain usable and Retry local copy is available. If primary data is unreadable, use the recovery or backup-import action shown by the app.

Watch installation and watch-face updates

Open Settings → Sync & devices → Apple Watch for installation and watch-face guides, connection status and the last received data. Install through the iPhone Watch app on a paired watch. You can refresh the connection; a requested check does not mean transfer is complete. Offline records wait on the watch for transfer. Confirm no records are pending before clearing the watch. The system controls watch-face and widget refresh timing.

Add Home Screen or Lock Screen widgets

Find the relevant guides under Settings → Help & about → Usage guides. Home Screen widgets show progress and can log water; Lock Screen widgets show progress and open the app when tapped. Complete first-time setup first. Open the app once after updating if it is missing from the widget list. Adding, removing and managing Lock Screen privacy happens through the system interface.

View the guide again or close it

Choose Settings → Help & about → Usage guides → View setup guide again. Each page of a reviewed guide can be closed to return to Help. First-time setup has no Close button. You still decide whether to allow Health and notifications in the system sheets; declining does not prevent local logging.

Health sync has not finished

Check system Health permissions and the in-app sync switch, unlock iPhone and retry on the Apple Health settings page. The app coordinates only Health entries it saved. The last successful time records a completed app sync, not confirmation that another device received system Health data. Turning sync off does not automatically delete existing Health entries.

Contact and support

Open Settings → Help & about → Contact & support → Email us, or email support@fenrir-inc.com.cn. Include the issue, app version, iOS/watchOS versions and reproduction steps. Redact personal or health information from screenshots. Never send passwords or unnecessary water history.

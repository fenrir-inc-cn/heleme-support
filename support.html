常见问题与使用指南

固定间隔和智能提醒如何选择？

在“设置 → 饮水与提醒 → 提醒计划”选择模式。固定模式沿用所选间隔；智能模式根据最近饮水事件的记录量调整下一周期；历史覆盖可靠且记录进度偏低时，最多提前基础间隔的 15%。没有新饮水事件时，后续常规提醒按基础间隔继续，同时遵守时段、每日上限和达标后停止选项。支持跨午夜时段，起止时间不能相同；15 分钟稍后提醒也计入同一额度。额度按已经过去的预约时点计数，不是已读通知次数，重新打开 App 不会重置。升级用户的原有固定计划不会自动切换。

消息中的“上班／出游”等只是按计划投递日期和时段选择的轻松文案，App 不读取你的行程、位置或实际活动。周末按本地日历判断，不识别法定节假日调休或个人班表。

在哪里看近两周饮水习惯洞察？

打开“历史 → 趋势 → 最近 → 近两周饮水习惯洞察”。分析最近 14 个完整本地自然日，今天从明天起纳入；均值按有记录的日期计算。记录不足或没有明确模式时会显示相应提示，不推断你没有喝水。四段条形图展示各饮水时段的记录量占比；空态表示没有足够记录，不表示实际饮水量为零。时区变化可能改变日期分组。

有依据且可执行的时段建议先展示预览，确认后才保存。最多启用一个上午／下午／晚间优化，优先移动一条已有提醒最多 15 分钟；必要时每天最多额外一次，仍计入同一上限，其他时段不变，晚间窗口最后 60 分钟不新增优化提醒。可在洞察页进入管理，或在“设置 → 饮水与提醒 → 提醒计划 → 习惯提醒优化”关闭。暂停或权限不可用时会显示未生效状态。切换为固定间隔后，优化不会调整提醒；已有策略保留查看和关闭，并显示“已暂停：仅适用于智能提醒”。没有已保存策略时，固定模式不显示新增优化入口，洞察统计和图表仍可查看。提醒和洞察只描述记录习惯，不是健康评分或医学要求。

以下说明对应 2.0 的功能与入口；较早安装版本可能有所不同。

洞察里的时间、变化比例和目标统计是什么意思？

补记按你填写的饮水发生时间统计，不按保存时间统计。空档说明展示上午／下午／晚间，表示该时段多次出现至少 3 小时的相邻饮水记录空档，并不证明没有喝水。周变化比较前后两个完整 7 天窗口各自有记录日期的日均量；每周至少 4 天有记录才比较，无记录日不按零值加入平均。目标统计只使用本版本起可核实的历史目标，同一天最后确认的目标生效；旧日期或换机后可能显示“暂无可比目标数据”。

为什么优化效果还不能评估？

启用后需完成至少 7 个完整自然日，并有足够可靠、可观察的记录与提醒状态。期间关闭、权限不可用、相关设置或时区改变，以及样本不足，都可能使评估不可用。前后对比显示该时段有记录天数／可观察天数和有记录日均量，不代表变化由提醒导致。优化、目标历史和效果观察仅由本机维护，不随本 App 的 iCloud 设置备份、Watch 同步或 JSON 记录备份迁移。

如何调整目标和常用杯量？

打开“设置 → 饮水与提醒 → 饮水目标与杯量”，调整每日饮水记录目标、参考选项和常用杯量。目标和杯量控件下方提供参考说明，并可打开“饮水建议与出处”查看建议和来源。1500／1700 mL 是可调整的参考选项，不计食物中的水分，不是针对个人的医疗建议。不要为达标集中饮水；有饮水限制时请遵循医嘱。

如何切换语言或深色模式？

打开“设置 → 外观与语言”。“外观模式”可选择“跟随系统”“浅色”或“深色”；“App 语言（系统设置）”打开 iOS 的本 App 设置。若系统没有显示语言选项，可先在“设置 → 通用 → 语言与地区”添加所需语言。系统选项名称可能因 iOS 版本而异，语言和外观偏好不属于完整记录备份。

首页的提醒时间和状态怎么看？

提醒关闭或通知权限不可用时，首页不再显示假想的提醒时间。暂停期间优先显示暂停截止时间，可直接点按“恢复提醒”；达到记录目标或不在提醒时段时会说明原因。只有查询确认已安排到系统的未来预约才显示为下一次提醒；实际送达仍由 iOS 管理。

为什么完成目标后，下次提醒是明天？

固定间隔模式在当天达到记录目标后停止当天提醒。智能模式由“达到目标后停止常规提醒”控制：开启时停止，关闭时仍遵守提醒时段和每日上限。时段结束、暂停或每日额度用尽也可能使下一次提醒顺延。请根据首页的实际状态查看原因，不要为了进度环而额外饮水。

没有收到提醒怎么办？

打开“设置 → 饮水与提醒 → 提醒计划”，检查通知开关、固定／智能模式、时段、每日上限和达标后停止选项。确认 iOS 允许通知，并查看首页是否暂停或处于非提醒时段。“设置 → 帮助与关于 → 使用指南 → 提醒测试与排查”提供“发送测试提醒”入口。专注模式、通知摘要及手机／手表路由可能影响提示；测试通知不是所有正式提醒均会准时送达的保证。

长时间不打开 App，还会提醒吗？

App 提前安排有限的一段时间，并在使用时续排，同时在系统允许时尝试后台续排。若需要你操作，会在计划结束前发送续排提示；打开 App、选择“继续提醒”或使用“刷新提醒”可重新安排。后台刷新和通知送达由 iOS 管理，无法保证永久无人操作或每次准时提示。

如何修改、补记、删除或导出记录？

在“历史 → 趋势”的最近、周、月、年查看图表，并在统计下方使用“查看记录”进入对应日期。固定版式可点按或水平拖动选点；需要滚动的版式通过点按选点，纵向滑动用于浏览页面。点按清除按钮取消选择。月历无论当天有无明细，都保留补记饮水按钮；点按记录可编辑，左滑可删除。CSV 导出入口在月历右上角；CSV 报表不能代替完整 JSON 备份。没有记录不代表没有喝水。

Apple 健康、iCloud 和完整备份有什么区别？

  • Apple 健康：可选同步本 App 的饮水记录。设置入口为“同步、备份与设备 → Apple 健康同步”，可查看状态、最近成功时间并手动同步；恢复健康记录需要主动确认。
  • App 内的 iCloud 备份：只备份目标、杯量和提醒设置，不包含饮水记录。入口为“同步、备份与设备 → iCloud 设置备份”。“把设置备份到 iCloud”不会创建完整饮水记录备份。
  • 完整文件备份：在“设置 → 同步、备份与设备 → 数据备份与恢复”导出或导入 JSON 文件，包含饮水记录与目标、杯量、提醒设置。

系统健康云同步与系统设备备份由 Apple 的设置管理，与以上 App 功能分开。语言和外观选择不包含在 App 内的 iCloud 设置备份或完整记录备份中。

换机备份入口与导出时间

打开“设置 → 同步、备份与设备 → 数据备份与恢复”，选择“导出完整备份文件”。新 iPhone 可在同一页面选择“导入备份文件”，恢复饮水记录和设置；iCloud 设置备份只恢复设置，Apple 健康同步另行管理。只有系统确认文件保存成功后才更新最近导出时间；该时间不能保证文件之后仍存在或可读取。请在确认新设备的记录完整后再抹掉旧设备。

如何导入完整备份？

在“数据备份与恢复”选择“导入备份文件”，选取本 App 导出的 JSON 文件，查看新增、更新、删除及设置变化的预览，确认后再导入。记录按编号和版本合并，并保留删除状态，避免重复计数或恢复已经删除的记录；备份中的目标、杯量和提醒设置会替换对应的当前设置。

单个文件上限为 20 MB，合并后最多保留 100,000 条记录，合并数据也需符合大小限制。无法读取、格式不兼容或超过限制时，App 会拒绝导入并保留本机数据。CSV 报表不能作为完整备份导入。

换机、卸载或抹掉设备前应做什么？

先导出完整 JSON 备份，保存在你信任的位置，并在新设备确认需要的记录已恢复。若使用 Apple 健康，也请检查 App 的健康同步状态和系统的健康备份／同步设置。导出文件包含饮水历史，App 不另设文件密码，请妥善保管；保存到 iCloud Drive 或其他文件服务取决于你在系统文件界面的选择。

“最近的本机副本”是什么？更新失败怎么办？

“本机恢复副本”保存在当前 iPhone 上，用于主数据无法读取时的恢复，不能代替设备外的备份。核对副本日期，较新的修改可能未包含其中。副本更新失败时，已经保存的主记录仍可使用，可点按“重新保存本机副本”；主数据无法读取时，请使用界面提供的恢复或备份导入入口。

手表上找不到 App，或表盘没有更新？

打开“设置 → 同步、备份与设备 → Apple Watch”，查看安装到手表、添加到表盘的指南、连接状态和最近收到的数据。安装需在 iPhone 的 Watch App 中完成，手机与手表须配对。可手动刷新连接；显示已请求检查不代表已经完成传输。临时断连时，记录会留在手表等待补传，请在清除手表前确认待同步数量归零。手表快速连点有一秒间隔保护，只有成功保存的新记录才触发记水振动；待确认数量表示手机尚未确认，不等同于已经同步完成。表盘和小组件的刷新时机由系统决定。手机桌面小组件显示快照更新时间，更新时间本身不代表跨设备传输成功；跨天按当天重新显示进度。

如何添加桌面或锁屏小组件？

在“设置 → 帮助与关于 → 使用指南”查看对应的添加指南。桌面小组件可显示进度并快捷记水；锁屏小组件显示进度，点按打开 App。先完成首次引导；更新后若组件列表未出现 App，请先打开 App 一次。添加、移除和锁屏隐私设置由系统界面管理。

可以重新查看或退出引导吗？

在“设置 → 帮助与关于 → 使用指南 → 重新查看引导”打开五步引导，依次确认 Apple 健康、iCloud 设置备份、提醒模式、提醒细节并完成。每个后续步骤都可返回前一步；重看时每页还可关闭。Apple 健康可以选择“暂不开启同步，继续”，iCloud 可以暂不备份设置；提醒页可调整间隔、起止时间和智能模式细节。拒绝可选权限不会阻止本地记水。首次引导没有关闭按钮。

健康同步一直未完成怎么办？

确认系统健康权限及 App 内同步开关，解锁 iPhone 后在 Apple 健康设置页重试。App 只协调自己写入的健康条目；最近成功时间只代表一次完整同步成功，不代表另一台设备已经收到系统健康数据。关闭同步不会自动删除已经写入健康的记录。

联系支持时提供什么？

在“设置 → 帮助与关于 → 联系与支持 → 通过邮件联系我们”打开邮件，或直接写信至 support@fenrir-inc.com.cn。请说明问题、App 版本、iOS／watchOS 版本和复现步骤。截图请先遮挡个人或健康信息；无需提供 Apple 账户密码或完整饮水记录。

Help and guides

Choose fixed or smart reminders

Choose the mode under Settings → Daily routine → reminder settings. Fixed mode uses your chosen interval. Smart mode adjusts the next cycle using the latest recorded drinking event. With reliable history and lower recorded progress, it may shorten the base interval by at most 15%. Without a new drinking event, later regular reminders use the base interval. Hours, the daily limit and goal-based stopping still apply. Hours may cross midnight, but start and end must differ. A 15-minute snooze shares the same daily allowance. The allowance counts elapsed reservations, not notification views, and reopening the app does not reset it. Upgrading does not automatically switch an existing fixed plan.

Work or outing themes are friendly wording chosen by the scheduled date and time, not information about your actual plans, location or activity. Weekends follow the local calendar, without recognizing holiday workday swaps or personal shifts.

Find insights from the past two weeks

Open History → Trends → Recent → Hydration insights · Past two weeks. Analysis covers the latest 14 complete local calendar days; today is included from tomorrow. Averages use days with entries. Insufficient data or no clear pattern is shown explicitly, without assuming that missing entries mean no drinking. The four time-of-day bars show shares of recorded volume; an empty state means insufficient records, not zero intake. Changing time zone can change date grouping.

A feasible period suggestion shows a preview before you confirm and save it. Only one morning, afternoon or evening optimization can be enabled. It first tries moving one reminder by at most 15 minutes; if needed, at most one extra per day shares the same daily limit. Other periods stay unchanged, and no extra evening optimization is added in the final 60 minutes of the window. Manage or close it from insights or Settings → Daily routine → Reminder schedule → Habit reminder optimization. Paused reminders or unavailable permission make the saved strategy inactive. In fixed-interval mode, optimization does not change reminders. A saved strategy remains available to view or close and is labeled “Paused: only available with smart reminders.” With no saved strategy, fixed mode hides optimization actions while keeping insight statistics and charts available. These findings are not a health score or medical requirement.

These instructions were checked against the current app interface on November 1, 2026. Availability and labels depend on your installed version; earlier versions may list equivalent controls directly on the main Settings screen.

Understand times, weekly changes and goal history

Manual entries use the drinking time you enter, not the save time. A morning/afternoon/evening gap finding means gaps of at least 3 hours recur between logged drinks; it does not prove no water was consumed. Weekly changes compare the daily averages of recorded days in two complete 7-day windows. Each week needs at least 4 recorded days; missing days are excluded, not counted as zero. Goal results use verifiable goals from this version onward, with the last confirmed goal for each day. Older dates or a device change may have no comparable goal history.

Why is there not enough evidence to evaluate optimization?

At least 7 complete calendar days after enabling and sufficiently reliable observations of records and reminder availability are needed. Closing, unavailable permission, relevant setting or time-zone changes, and insufficient records can prevent evaluation. Comparisons show recorded days / observable days and average volume on recorded days for that period; they do not prove a reminder caused the change. Optimization, goal history and observations are maintained locally and are not migrated through this app’s iCloud preferences backup, Watch sync or JSON record backup.

Change the goal and usual cup size

Open Settings → Daily routine → Water goal and cup size. Adjust the recorded-water goal, reference choices and usual cup size. Read the guidance below the controls and its source links. Reference goals exclude food water and are not personalized medical advice. Do not drink extra simply to complete the ring; follow professional advice if your intake is restricted.

Choose a language or Dark appearance

Open Settings → Appearance & language. Appearance mode offers Follow System, Light and Dark. App language (system settings) opens the app’s iOS settings. If the language option is absent, add the language under iOS Settings → General → Language & Region first. System labels vary by iOS version. Language and appearance choices are not part of the full record backup.

Understand the reminder status

The home screen hides hypothetical reminder times when reminders are off or notification permission is unavailable. While paused, it prioritizes the pause end time and offers Resume reminders. It also explains completed recording goals and times outside reminder hours. Only a future reservation confirmed in the system queue is shown as the next reminder; iOS controls actual delivery.

Why is the next reminder tomorrow after reaching the goal?

Fixed reminders stop for the day when the recording goal is reached. Smart reminders follow Stop regular reminders after reaching the goal: when it is off, they may continue within your chosen hours and daily limit. Quiet hours, a pause or the daily allowance can also move a reminder to a later day. Read the reason on Today rather than inferring it from the date. Do not drink extra to complete the ring.

No reminder?

Check Settings → Daily routine → Reminder schedule for notification permission, mode, hours, daily limit and goal-based stopping. Check whether reminders are paused on Today. Settings → Help & about → Usage guides → Reminder test & troubleshooting offers Send a test reminder. Focus, summaries and iPhone/Watch routing affect delivery. A successful test does not guarantee every scheduled reminder will arrive on time.

What if I stop opening the app?

The app schedules a finite period in advance and renews it when used, with background renewal when iOS permits. A renewal notice asks you to open the app or choose Continue reminders before the schedule ends. Refresh reminders can also renew the schedule. iOS controls background opportunities and delivery; unattended indefinite or exact-time alerts cannot be guaranteed.

Edit, add, delete or export water records

History → Trends includes Recent, Week, Month and Year, with View records below the totals. Fixed layouts support tapping or horizontal dragging to inspect; scrolling layouts use tapping so vertical swipes can scroll the page. Use the clear button to deselect. The calendar always keeps the manual-add action, including days with existing entries. Tap an entry to edit or swipe to delete. CSV export is at the calendar’s top right; a CSV report is not a restorable JSON backup. Missing entries do not mean no water was consumed.

Apple Health, iCloud and full backups

  • Apple Health: optionally syncs this app's water records. Under Sync, backups & devices → Apple Health sync, check status, the last successful sync time or sync manually. Restoring Health records requires confirmation.
  • In-app iCloud backup: stores goal, cup and reminder preferences only, without water records. Find it under Sync, backups & devices → iCloud Settings Backup. Back up settings to iCloud does not create a full record backup.
  • Full file backup: export or import a JSON file under Settings → Sync, backups & devices → Data backup & restore. It contains water records and goal, cup and reminder settings.

System Health cloud sync and system device backups are managed by Apple separately. Language and appearance choices are not included in the app's iCloud preferences backup or full record backup.

Before changing phones and the last export time

Open Settings → Sync, backups & devices → Data backup & restore and choose Export full backup file. On the new iPhone, use Import backup file on the same page to restore records and settings. iCloud Settings Backup restores settings only; Apple Health sync is managed separately. The last export time updates only after the system confirms saving. It does not prove that the file remains readable or available later. Confirm the needed records on the new device before erasing the old one.

Import a full backup

Choose Import backup file under Data backup & restore and select a JSON file exported by Heleme. Review the changes to records and settings before confirming. Records merge by identifier and version while preserving deletion state to avoid duplicates or restoring deleted entries. Imported goal, cup and reminder settings replace their current counterparts.

A file can be up to 20 MB; the merged data must also fit the size limit and contain at most 100,000 records. Unreadable, incompatible or over-limit imports are rejected while local data is retained. A CSV report cannot be imported as a full backup.

Before replacing, erasing or uninstalling

Export a full JSON backup to a trusted location and confirm the records you need have been restored on the new device. If you use Apple Health, also check app sync and the system's Health backup/sync settings. Exported files contain water history and have no extra app-managed password. Saving to iCloud Drive or another file service depends on your choice in the system file interface.

What is the latest local copy? What if it cannot update?

The Local recovery copy stays on this iPhone and helps when primary data cannot be read; it does not replace an external backup. Check its date because later changes may be missing. If only the copy failed to update, saved primary records remain usable and Retry saving local copy is available. If primary data is unreadable, use the recovery or backup-import action shown by the app.

Watch installation and watch-face updates

Open Settings → Sync, backups & devices → Apple Watch for installation and watch-face guides, connection status and the last received data. Install through the iPhone Watch app on a paired watch. You can refresh the connection; a requested check does not mean transfer is complete. Offline records wait on the watch for transfer. Confirm no records are pending before clearing the watch. A one-second guard prevents accidental rapid Watch taps. Logging feedback occurs only for a newly saved entry; pending entries have not yet been acknowledged by the phone. The system controls watch-face and widget refresh timing. Home Screen widgets show when their snapshot was updated. That timestamp is not proof of cross-device delivery; progress is calculated for the current day.

Add Home Screen or Lock Screen widgets

Find the relevant guides under Settings → Help & about → Usage guides. Home Screen widgets show progress and can log water; Lock Screen widgets show progress and open the app when tapped. Complete first-time setup first. Open the app once after updating if it is missing from the widget list. Adding, removing and managing Lock Screen privacy happens through the system interface.

View the guide again or close it

Open Settings → Help & about → Usage guides → View setup guide again. The five steps cover Apple Health, iCloud settings backup, reminder mode, reminder details and completion. Later steps have Back; a reviewed guide can also be closed on any page. You may skip Health sync or iCloud backup, and adjust intervals, start/end times and smart reminder details. Declining optional permissions does not prevent local logging. First-time setup has no Close button.

Health sync has not finished

Check system Health permissions and the in-app sync switch, unlock iPhone and retry on the Apple Health settings page. The app coordinates only Health entries it saved. The last successful time records a completed app sync, not confirmation that another device received system Health data. Turning sync off does not automatically delete existing Health entries.

Contact and support

Open Settings → Help & about → Contact & feedback → Email feedback, or email support@fenrir-inc.com.cn. Include the issue, app version, iOS/watchOS versions and reproduction steps. Redact personal or health information from screenshots. Never send passwords or unnecessary water history.

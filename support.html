4.0 帮助与指南

1. 外观风格与首次引导

设置 → 外观与语言 → 外观风格，可选择标准、可爱或清新，并分别管理水滴语音、喝水音效和外观模式。风格切换不改变记录。iPhone 新安装引导和首次升级到 4.0 的介绍提供独立主题选择页；可从「了解 4.0」重看升级介绍。清新主题按本地时间显示早上、中午、下午、傍晚与夜晚。

2. 今天：选饮品、记一杯与撤销

六种图标切换白水、茶、咖啡、牛奶、果汁和其他饮品。主按钮记录当前饮品与杯量，进度按实际容量计算，不做吸收率换算。记录后可撤销；历史可补记、更正和删除。更改常用设定仅保存饮品与杯量偏好，不新增饮水记录，弹窗取消不应用修改。

3. 杯量与目标

更改常用设定与设置 → 饮水目标与杯量共用配置。四个快捷杯量默认 100／200／300／400 mL，其中一个作为常用杯量，默认 200 mL；首页显示全部四项。标准版点快捷杯量直接记录，可爱与清新版先切换杯量，再点主按钮记录。设置页点选卡片只选择编辑项，点“设为常用”才改变常用选择。点减号、加号之间的数字可直接输入。四个杯量须互不重复且为 1–1,000 mL 整数；每日目标为 500–3,000 mL，单次新记录为 1–1,000 mL。这是输入限制，不是医学推荐量或安全界线。

4. iPhone 水滴互动、语音和音效

可爱和清新水滴保留挥手、喝水、休息与达标跳跃。选饮品可举杯，饮料颜色随种类变化。达标时在语音开启的情况下播放庆祝短句；撤销后重新达标会再次庆祝，已达标后普通加水不重复。

第一次点击水滴朗读首页提示，第二次复述最近 24 小时内实际送达的有效提醒，没有近期提醒时使用通用提醒；之后随机播放适合当前时段的短句，提醒语音不读杯量。语音和三咕咚音效完全离线。支持简繁中文、日语、英语、韩语、法语、西班牙语、葡萄牙语、德语、俄语和意大利语；越南语、泰语、阿拉伯语、丹麦语、挪威语、瑞典语、印地语和印尼语暂不提供水滴语音。

切换到可爱或清新时默认开启语音与音效；随后可分别关闭。没有声音时，检查这两个开关、系统静音、音量及音频输出设备；标准版没有互动水滴语音。简洁反馈、减少动态效果、VoiceOver 和后台状态会限制动画或朗读。

5. 成长、奖章与每周回顾

成长展示有记录天数、本周记录与奖章。我的奖章包含累计与持续记录里程碑，获得时间根据记录计算；修改记录可能改变结果。进入奖章页会确认本次可见的新奖章提示，新增里程碑仍会提示。起身活动由手动完成记录，不是自动检测。

每周回顾默认显示最近四周，向下滚动加载更早周次，七天按周日到周六排列。展示有记录天数、达标天数、仅对有记录日期计算的日均记录值，以及本周记录量最多的三种饮品和其余饮品汇总；无记录不作零饮水推断。

6. 历史目标与近两周洞察

每日适用目标从 4.0 起纳入完整 iCloud 同步和 JSON 备份。升级前缺少目标历史的日期使用升级当天目标作为估算，页面会说明；这不会改写实际饮水记录。重装前确认 iCloud 同步成功或导出完整 JSON，重装后恢复再核对。

近两周洞察分析今天之前的 14 天，展示有记录天数、达标天数、日均记录值、时段分布与可用观察。日均只计算有记录的日期，缺记录不等于没有喝水。洞察不是健康结论。

7. 提醒计划与优化

设置 → 提醒计划可管理固定或智能模式、时段、间隔与每日上限，支持跨午夜且起止时间不能相同。智能模式可按记录习惯缩短基础间隔，最多 15%。可用优化先调整一条最多 15 分钟，必要时每日最多增加一次，仍受每日上限约束；需要确认，固定模式不应用优化。

今天的下次提醒菜单与成长洞察可管理可用优化。暂停、达标停止、时段结束或额度用完可能令下次提醒出现在明天或之后，请阅读时间下方原因。检查系统权限、专注模式与暂停设置；排程失败可刷新。系统决定实际送达和 iPhone／Watch 路由，测试通知成功不代表所有正式提醒或快捷操作已验证。

8. iCloud、Apple 健康与文件备份

设置 → 跨设备同步合并同一 Apple 账户下已开启同步的记录与设置。Watch 通过配对 iPhone 同步。切换账户后同步暂停，先核对账户；关闭同步不删除云端记录。旧设置备份不包含全部记录，仅在本机尚未成功完成新版同步时显示恢复入口。

在 iPhone 的引导或设置 → Apple 健康开启连接后，按需选择白水、站立小时和步数的权限。仅白水记录会写入 Apple 健康；其他饮品保留在 App 和可选 iCloud 同步中。站立小时和步数为只读，提供本周活动回顾和可选的健康辅助提醒。曾拒绝权限时，打开系统「健康」→ 头像 → 隐私 → App（或 App 与服务）→ 喝了么，重新选择允许的项目。在喝了么的设置 → Apple 健康中可停止使用活动数据并清除本机摘要。饮水记录先保存在 App；健康同步失败可重试。

数据备份与恢复可导出、预览并导入完整 JSON；重复导入不会增加相同记录。4.0 的历史适用目标、升级目标基准、起身活动及奖章已读状态随记录数据备份与恢复。iPhone 导出的文件还包含界面风格、外观模式、水滴语音与喝水音效开关，导入前可核对，确认恢复成功后生效；已关闭的开关会原样保留。旧备份缺少外观字段时不改变本机外观。iCloud 同步记录数据，各设备的外观设置仍独立保留。导入上限 20 MB，合并后最多 100000 条；CSV 仅为报表。本机恢复副本不能代替外部备份。抹掉旧设备前，在新设备实际核对各饮品、目标历史与设置。

9. 历史、屏幕尺寸与语言

iPhone 上可在趋势和月历间切换，查看最近、周、月、年；点图表看数值，明细可编辑、删除及补记。iPhone Pro 优先提供舒适的首屏布局，小屏和辅助大字号允许滚动；Duo 使用专门布局。App 语言跟随系统，可从设置中的语言入口打开 iOS 设置。

10. Apple Watch 与小组件

自动安装：在 iPhone 打开「Watch → 我的手表 → 通用」，开启「自动安装 App」。手动安装：在「Watch → 我的手表 → 可用 App」里找到喝了么，轻点「安装」。离线记录等待手机确认，清理手表前先确认待同步项。小组件提供常用或预设杯量与收藏入口，能力和刷新时机由系统决定；锁屏或表盘可能显示记录，请留意隐私。

11. Mac 4.0

Mac 主窗口从上到下显示今日记录、趋势、月历与成长。选择月历日期可查看、补记和修改当天记录。可从 App 菜单或按 ⌘, 打开设置，在外观风格中选择标准、可爱或清新，分别控制离线水滴语音与喝水音效。

在可爱或清新风格下，用首页的快捷记录保存饮品，3D 水滴会举杯喝水。点击水滴会挥手。开启水滴语音且当前语言支持时，还可以听到离线问候。首次达到当天目标时，水滴会庆祝。如果水滴保持静止，请检查「设置 → 轻柔反馈」是否选了「简洁反馈」，或 macOS 是否开启了「减少动态效果」。

首次打开会显示设置引导。可从「设置 → 帮助与关于」再次查看，重看时可以随时关闭。切换引导页后底部按钮短暂禁用，等按钮启用再继续。在 macOS 桌面编辑小组件并添加喝了么，可查看今日进度或快捷记录。可在设置中启用跨设备同步，同一 Apple 账户的启用设备可合并饮水记录。Mac 不直接访问 Apple 健康。

12. 遇到问题与联系

若数据无法读取，先使用 App 提供的恢复、重试或备份入口，不要立即卸载。联系 support@fenrir-inc.com.cn 时提供平台、App／系统版本及复现步骤，并遮挡截图敏感信息。不要发送 Apple 账户密码或不必要的完整记录。

4.0 help and guides

1. Appearance and onboarding

Settings → Appearance & language → Appearance style offers Standard, Cute and Fresh, plus separate droplet speech, drinking sound and appearance-mode controls. Styles do not change records. On iPhone, first-run setup and the first 4.0 upgrade introduction include a dedicated style page. Reopen the upgrade introduction in Discover 4.0. Fresh follows five local time periods from morning to night.

2. Today: choose, record and undo

Six icons select water, tea, coffee, milk, juice and other drinks. The main button logs the selected drink and amount. Progress uses actual logged volume without absorption conversion. Undo recent entries; backfill, correct or delete in History. Change usual settings saves drink/cup preferences without adding a record. Cancel discards the draft.

3. Cups and goals

Change usual settings and Settings → Water goal and cup size share one configuration. Four quick cups start at 100/200/300/400 mL; one is usual, initially 200 mL, and Today shows all four. In Standard, tapping a quick cup records it immediately; in Cute and Fresh, it selects the amount for the main record button. Selecting a settings card chooses what to edit; Set as usual changes the usual selection. Tap the number between minus and plus to type. Cups must be distinct integers from 1–1,000 mL. Goals accept 500–3,000 mL and new entries 1–1,000 mL. These are input limits, not medical recommendations or safety thresholds.

4. Droplet interactions, speech and sounds on iPhone

Cute and Fresh droplets wave, drink, rest and jump on reaching a goal. Selecting a drink raises a cup and changes its liquid color. With speech enabled, the goal jump includes a happy phrase. Undoing and reaching the goal again celebrates again; additional drinks while already above the goal do not repeat it.

The first tap reads Today’s message; the second repeats the latest valid reminder actually delivered in the last 24 hours, or uses a general reminder if none exists. Later taps choose phrases suitable for the current time. Spoken reminders omit cup amounts. Speech and the three-gulp sound are entirely offline. Speech supports Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Japanese, English, Korean, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Russian and Italian. Vietnamese, Thai, Arabic, Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, Hindi and Indonesian currently have no droplet speech.

Changing to Cute or Fresh enables speech and drinking sounds by default; each can then be turned off. If silent, check both switches, system silent mode, volume and audio output. Standard has no interactive droplet speech. Simple feedback, Reduce Motion, VoiceOver and background state can limit animation or speech.

5. Growth, medals and weekly recaps

Growth shows recorded days, this week and medals. My medals includes cumulative and sustained recording milestones. Earned dates are derived from records and can change after corrections. Opening the medal page acknowledges its current new-medal notices; later milestones can generate new notices. Movement breaks are manually completed, not automatically detected.

Weekly recap starts with the latest four weeks; scroll down to load earlier weeks. Days run Sunday to Saturday. Cards show recorded days, goal days and daily average over recorded dates only. Drink breakdown uses the week’s three largest drink categories plus the remainder. Missing entries do not prove zero intake.

6. Historical goals and two-week insights

Starting in 4.0, applicable daily goals participate in full iCloud sync and JSON backups. Earlier dates missing goal history use the upgrade-day goal as an explicitly disclosed estimate; actual drink entries are unchanged. Before reinstalling, verify iCloud sync or export full JSON, then restore and check it afterward.

Insights analyze the 14 days before today, showing recorded days, goal days, daily average, time distribution and available observations. The average excludes dates without records. Missing entries do not prove no drinking, and insights are not health conclusions.

7. Reminder schedules and optimization

Settings → Reminder schedule manages fixed/smart mode, hours, intervals and daily limits. Overnight hours are supported; start/end must differ. Smart mode can shorten the base interval by at most 15%. Available optimization first shifts one reminder by up to 15 minutes and may add at most one reminder per day within the daily limit. Confirmation is required; fixed mode does not apply optimization.

Manage available optimization through Today’s next-reminder menu or Growth insights. Pauses, goal-based stopping, quiet hours and daily allowances can move the next alert to tomorrow or later; read the reason below its time. Check system permissions, Focus and pause settings; refresh after scheduling errors. The system controls delivery and phone/Watch routing. A successful test alert does not validate every normal reminder or action.

8. iCloud, Apple Health and backup files

Cross-device sync merges records and settings for enabled devices on the same Apple Account. Watch uses its paired iPhone. Account changes pause sync; check the account first. Disabling sync does not delete cloud records. Legacy settings backups lack complete records and their restore area appears only until this device successfully completes the new sync.

Connect from onboarding or Settings → Apple Health on iPhone and choose the permissions you want for water, stand hours and steps. Only plain water is written to Health. Other drinks stay in Heleme and its optional iCloud sync. Stand hours and steps are read-only and support the weekly activity recap and optional Health-assisted reminders. If you previously denied access, open the system Health app → your profile → Privacy → Apps (or Apps and Services) → Heleme and allow the types you want. Stop Using Activity Data under Settings → Apple Health clears activity summaries from this iPhone. Water entries are saved in Heleme first; you can retry failed Health sync.

Data backup & restore exports, previews and imports full JSON. Repeated import does not duplicate the same records. Version 4.0 backs up and restores applicable goal history, the upgrade goal baseline, movement and medal read status with record data. Files exported on iPhone also include visual style, appearance mode, droplet speech and drinking-sound switches. Review these before import; they apply after successful restoration, preserving switches saved as off. Older files without appearance fields leave local appearance unchanged. iCloud syncs record data while each device retains its own appearance settings. Limits are 20 MB per import and 100,000 merged records. CSV is only a report; a local recovery copy is not an external backup. Verify all drink types, goal history and settings on the new device before erasing the old one.

9. History, display sizes and language

On iPhone, History switches between Trends and Calendar for recent, week, month and year views. Tap charts to inspect values; edit, delete or backfill in details. iPhone Pro is the primary comfortable one-screen layout; smaller screens and accessibility text can scroll, while Duo has a dedicated layout. App language follows system settings; the language entrance opens iOS Settings.

10. Apple Watch and widgets

For automatic installation, open Watch → My Watch → General on iPhone and turn on Automatic App Install. To install manually, find Heleme under Watch → My Watch → Available Apps and tap Install. Offline entries await phone acknowledgement; check pending items before clearing Watch. Widgets offer usual/preset cups and favorites. Capabilities and refresh timing depend on the system. Lock Screen or watch-face displays may expose records.

11. Mac 4.0

The Mac window shows Today, trends, the calendar and Growth in that order. Select a calendar date to view, add or edit entries for that day. Open Settings from the app menu or press Command–Comma. Under Appearance style, choose Standard, Cute or Fresh and control offline droplet speech and drinking sounds separately.

In Cute or Fresh, save a drink with Quick record to see the 3D droplet lift its cup and drink. Click the droplet to make it wave. You can also hear an offline greeting if droplet speech is enabled and your language is supported. The droplet celebrates when you first reach the daily goal. If the droplet stays still, check whether Simple feedback is selected under Settings → Gentle feedback or Reduce Motion is enabled in macOS.

First launch opens the setup guide. Reopen it under Settings → Help & about and close it whenever you like. After changing guide pages, wait for the bottom buttons to become active. Add a Heleme widget from the macOS desktop widget gallery for progress or quick logging. Turn on cross-device sync in Settings to merge drink records across enabled devices using the same Apple Account. Mac does not access Apple Health directly.

12. Troubleshooting and contact

If data cannot be read, use the app’s recovery, retry or backup options before uninstalling. Email support@fenrir-inc.com.cn with platform, app/system versions and reproduction steps. Redact sensitive screenshots; do not send Apple Account passwords or unnecessary full records.
